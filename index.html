Meet the chef

Chef Andre “Dre” Christie

Chef Dre's journey began in Jamaica, in the kitchen of his grandmother, Miss Mavis. It was his first classroom, where he learned the value of seasoning, patience, family and putting care into every meal.

That passion grew into more than 20 years of culinary experience, including time alongside chefs from the renowned Sandals Resorts. Today he leads Dre's Island Food Services, catering upscale private parties, weddings and galas across Florida.

  • Authentic Jamaican recipes and creative culture-fusion menus
  • Hands-on communication from first call to final plate
  • 2025 Best Startup award nominee, Pasco County EDC

Good to know

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Call (772) 475-4460 or send us a note below.

Where do you cater?

We're based in Pasco County and cater events throughout Tampa Bay and Central Florida, including Wesley Chapel, Dade City, Tampa and Brandon. Planning something further away? Ask us.

How is pricing determined?

Every event is custom-quoted based on your guest count, menu and service style. Share a few details in the form and we'll send a personalized quote.

How far in advance should we book?

As early as you can, especially for weddings and holiday dates. We've also come through on last-minute requests, so it's always worth asking.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes. We regularly create cultural and fusion dishes around specific dietary needs. Let us know about any allergies when you request your quote.

Do you cater weddings and large events?

Absolutely. We've catered weddings and galas for up to 250 guests, as well as corporate luncheons, nonprofit parties and intimate private dinners.
