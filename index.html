Our story

We Don't Just Cater Food, We Cater an experience.

Our foundation is simple: flavorful island dishes, built on a blend of authentic spices, and a real attention to detail in every one of them.

We put the same care into how the food looks and how it is served. Presentation and front-line service are part of the dish, so you get a complete experience, the Dre's Island way. We have curated it around one idea: bringing the best from us to you.

From our founder, Chef Dre, to every member of our front-line team, we put passion and creativity into each event so it becomes an exquisite, memorable occasion. We are confident you will enjoy it, and that your guests will tell others about it.
